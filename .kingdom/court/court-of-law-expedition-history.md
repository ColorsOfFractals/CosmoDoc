# I. WHY THIS RECORD EXISTS

This document is not a proposal for how Kingdom Builder ought to work.

It is not doctrine written ahead of experience.

It is the archaeological record of the institution that actually survived the case.

The Court of Law was not designed in finished form and then imposed upon CosmoDoc. It assembled recursively because the construction problem demanded increasingly explicit boundaries between building, evidence, judgment, admission, recovery, and accepted reality.

The problem summoned the organization it required.

Kingdom Builder began with a simple governing instinct:

> Protect the known-good center while allowing construction to happen around it.

That instinct became a system.

A FOSSIL preserved known-good time.

A SPIRAL bounded one construction effort.

A TORNADO allowed several spirals to develop around the protected center.

A WATCHER made work observable.

The EYE became independent acceptance authority.

A STITCH became deterministic admission.

TORNADO REPAIR became the correction and convergence mechanism.

The KINGDOM became the integrated reality that survived all of those gates.

And the COURT OF LAW emerged when construction itself became complex enough that claims about success, failure, authority, and history needed judgment.

The Court therefore records not merely what was built.

It records how the Kingdom learned what it was allowed to believe.

# II. THE CONSTITUTION THAT EMERGED

The following principles were not granted authority merely because they sounded useful. They survived construction, failure, repair, inspection, or historical review.

## Protect the center

The known-good Kingdom is not the workshop.

Possible futures are constructed around it.

A failed child, failed simulation, failed test bench, failed survey, or failed judgment must not be allowed to steal the accepted world.

Failure is made cheap by making it local.

## Fossils give us time. Tornadoes give us space.

A fossil provides a recoverable historical coordinate.

A Tornado permits independent construction without forcing every unfinished possibility into the same mutable workspace.

Together they separate chronology from concurrency.

## The Eye gives us truth.

Worker completion is testimony.

A fossil plus reproducible validation is evidence.

Eye inspection determines whether evidence is sufficient for admission.

The Stitch incorporates only what the Eye authorized.

The Kingdom contains only what survived admission.

## Children define themselves; the Eye defines relationships.

A child spiral may own its internal construction.

It does not own the meaning of its relationship to the rest of the Kingdom.

Integration authority belongs above the child.

## Repair may not certify itself.

A repair pass may correct a defect.

It does not gain authority merely because it completed.

The same jurisdiction that discovered the defect must be able to reinspect the repaired reality.

## The machinery that builds the Kingdom must never become part of the Kingdom by accident.

Construction machinery, simulations, test chambers, temporary worktrees, generated harnesses, and orchestration artifacts require containment.

The product and the factory are related.

They are not the same object.

## Topology before reinforcement.

Before strengthening a worker, module, or territory, determine whether the work is shaped correctly.

Removing a BLOCK does not imply BUILD.

It reveals the next judgment the BLOCK was hiding.

## Work does not need to be linear. Dependencies do.

The Fractal Tornado asks:

> What is the maximum amount of independent construction this dependency graph permits while still giving deterministic convergence?

Organization therefore follows the shape of the problem.

The problem is not forced through a permanent organization.

## Move consequences later than discovery.

Survey before mutation.

Simulate before production authority.

Construct before admission.

Inspect before stitch.

Preserve evidence before interpretation.

This became the CNC principle of Kingdom Builder:

> Move consequences later than discovery.

# XVI. EVIDENCE LAW

The Court leaves behind a hierarchy of epistemic authority.

## Testimony

A worker state, landing message, or human recollection can tell the Court what someone believed happened.

Testimony matters.

It is not automatically proof.

## Evidence

Git objects, patch identities, source blobs, reproducible builds, runtime results, preserved artifacts, explicit coordinates, and clean-worktree proofs can support factual claims.

Evidence may itself require calibration.

## Judgment

Evidence does not interpret itself.

A judge evaluates a bounded question under an explicit constitution.

Judgment must remain reproducible and reviewable.

## Eye authority

The Eye independently decides whether a claimed reality may be admitted.

The Eye does not become infallible.

The Eye becomes accountable to evidence.

## Stitch

The Stitch performs authorized convergence.

It does not decide semantics.

It does not invent authority.

It incorporates what was admitted.

## Kingdom reality

Only after construction, evidence, judgment, admission, and deterministic convergence does material become part of the accepted Kingdom.

This yields the durable formulation:

> Worker state is testimony. Fossil plus reproducible validation is evidence.

And:

> A landing message is testimony. Git history and ancestry are evidence.

And:

> We do not patch. We prove. We converge.
